ZAP!

You were on a geography field trip, stomping about in a brook in your wellies, when you trod on Gravel Gary’s cousin’s homemade shrink ray. You are now the size of a pebble, stuck at the source of a very blocky river.

The only way home is downstream: through the upper course, the middle course and the lower course, all the way to the mouth. Every room is sealed by golden, sparkly locks. Answer a geography question to open each one, collect the three Sediment Keys and ride out to sea.

Gravel Gary (an anxious pebble), Professor Puddle (a very pompous frog) and Rex (a retired dinosaur) will help. Mostly.

How to play

  1. Move: WASD or arrow keys. Look: move the mouse. Jump: Space. On a tablet, use the joystick and drag the screen to look.
  2. Find the golden sparkles. Look at a lock and press E or click (tablet: USE).
  3. Open all 5 locks in a room and the door to the next room opens.
  4. Multiple choice: 2 tries. Typed answers: 3 tries. Help shows your notes, but that question then won’t count as right first time.
  5. Esc pauses. Walk into the glowing mouth of the river to escape!

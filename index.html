A BROOK · A FIELD TRIP · ONE VERY SMALL PROBLEM

You were on a geography field trip, stomping about in a brook in your wellies, when you trod on Gravel Gary’s cousin’s homemade shrink ray. You are now the size of a pebble, stuck at the source of a very blocky river.

The only way home is downstream: through the upper course, the middle course and the lower course, all the way to the mouth. A log jam, a sluice gate and a flood barrier block the way. Open the golden chests to collect the three Sediment Keys, then wade out to sea.

Gravel Gary (an anxious pebble), Professor Puddle (a very pompous frog) and Rex (a retired dinosaur) will help. Mostly.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Follow the river downhill. Every course of the river looks different: a steep V-shaped valley with a waterfall, then meanders and an oxbow lake, then a wide floodplain with levees.
  2. ✦ Golden sparkles mark a chest. Look at it and press E (tablet: USE) to answer a geography question.
  3. Read the wooden signs. Each one names the landform in front of you, in words from your booklet.
  4. Open all 5 chests in a course and the way downstream opens. Multiple choice: 2 tries. Typed: 3 tries. Help shows your notes, but then that question won’t count as right first time.
  5. At the mouth, walk into the sea to escape!

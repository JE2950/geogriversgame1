# Source to Sea: The Great Pebble Escape

A Minecraft-style 3D escape room for Year 7 Geography: Rivers. Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). It works on laptops and tablets, and nothing needs installing.

You have been shrunk to the size of a pebble at the source of a river. Follow it downhill to the sea through four areas:

| Area | Landforms you walk through | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| I. The Upper Course Gorge | Source, V-shaped valley, interlocking spurs, waterfall with overhang and plunge pool, gorge, rapids | Log jam |
| II. The Meander Meadows | Tributary and confluence, meanders, river cliff and slip-off slope, oxbow lake | Sluice gate |
| III. The Levee Lowlands | Floodplain, levees, sandbags, a concrete dike, a village and farmland | Flood barrier |
| IV. The Delta and the Mouth | Delta channels, reeds and the sea | Walk into the sea to escape |

- Open the 5 golden chests in each area by answering a question. Multiple choice gets 2 tries and typed answers get 3, with feedback on each wrong answer.
- Wooden signs name each landform, using the words from the booklet.
- **Help** shows the topic notes, but that question then can't count as right first time.
- At the end, a fly-through of the river's long profile labels every landform. Then the end screen shows stars, a list of things to practise grouped by topic, and every answer.

Teachers can choose which topics and question types to include on the title screen. These choices are remembered in the browser, and no personal data is stored. There are 62 questions across 8 topics, and each game picks 15, avoiding the previous game's questions where possible.

**Controls:** WASD to walk, mouse to look, Space to jump, Shift to run, E or click to use, Esc to pause. On a tablet, use the left stick, drag to look, and tap USE.
